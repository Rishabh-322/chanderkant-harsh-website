Meet Your Healer

Where clinical method meets lived awakening

Chanderkant Harsh is a Mind Healer and Transformational Therapist whose practice sits at the meeting point of science and spirituality. Through Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP) and subconscious mind reprogramming (hypnosis), he has guided people out of patterns of pain and into lives built on clarity and purpose.

He works with people carrying emotional weight, physical and psychosomatic conditions, relationship strain, and addiction, helping them read the hidden scripts running underneath the surface of daily life.

Every life is a mirror of the beliefs held in the subconscious. Change the belief, and the life it reflects begins to change too.

His mission is direct: replace limiting subconscious beliefs with empowering ones, and awaken the healer already present within every client.
